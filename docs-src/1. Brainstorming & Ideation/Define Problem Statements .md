## Define Problem Statements (Customer Problem Statement Template)

PocketSmart AI helps people who must spend a fixed amount of money across many products and services and cannot tell whether a plan fits the budget until after they have browsed several platforms.

| Problem Statement (PS) | I am (Customer) | I'm trying to | But | Because | Which makes me feel |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PS-1 | A first-time home owner furnishing a flat | Buy lights, ceiling fans, furniture and a dining table within a fixed budget | I overspend on one category and run short on others | Prices differ on IKEA, Amazon and Flipkart and I have no single view of the total | Anxious about overspending |
| PS-2 | A host planning a birthday or family party | Split my budget between venue, catering, decoration and entertainment | I cannot tell how much each part should cost for my guest count | Quotes are scattered across Swiggy, Zomato, OYO and decor shops | Stressed and unsure |
| PS-3 | A shopper buying jewelry for a wedding or festival | Find pieces that match my outfit and stay within budget | I cannot judge what suits my outfit colour and style | Catalogues are huge and show no budget-aware or outfit-aware guidance | Overwhelmed |
| PS-4 | A busy student or young professional | Get a ready, shop-able plan in minutes | I waste hours comparing sites | There is no one assistant that plans across categories and platforms | Relieved if the plan is done for me |
