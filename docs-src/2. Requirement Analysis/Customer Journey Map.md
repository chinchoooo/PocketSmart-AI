## Customer Journey Map

| Phase of Journey | Stage 1: Discover & Sign Up | Stage 2: Plan the Budget | Stage 3: Shop & Revisit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Actions** <br> What does the customer do? | - Opens the landing page<br>- Reads about the three planners<br>- Registers and signs in | - Chooses Home, Party or Jewelry planner<br>- Enters budget, quantities, occasion and optional outfit photo<br>- Submits the form | - Reviews category totals and remaining budget<br>- Opens platform links to buy<br>- Returns later via History |
| **Touchpoint** <br> What part of the service do they interact with? | - Landing page<br>- Register and Login pages | - Planner forms<br>- Gemini-powered recommendation engine | - Results page with shop links<br>- Dashboard and History pages |
| **Customer Thought** <br> What is the customer thinking? | - "Will this really fit my budget?"<br>- "Is signing up quick?" | - "Will the AI understand my needs?"<br>- "Is my photo used safely?" | - "Great, nothing exceeds my budget."<br>- "I can find this plan again." |
| **Customer Feeling** <br> What is the customer feeling? | Curious, slightly sceptical | Hopeful, a little impatient while waiting | Relieved and confident |
| **Process Ownership** <br> Who is in the lead on this? | Frontend and Auth module (Sanjay C, Kaushal N) | AI service and Backend (Krishna Kailash Yedida, Aravinth K) | Frontend, History and QA (Kaushal N, Rangesh V S) |
| **Opportunities** <br> How can we improve this stage? | - Add Google sign-in<br>- Add a guest demo mode | - Add budget presets and templates<br>- Stream results as they are generated | - Add price-drop alerts<br>- Share or export a plan as PDF |
